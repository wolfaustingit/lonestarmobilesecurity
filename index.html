FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can you get a tower on my site?

Most Greater Houston deployments can be scheduled within days of signing your lease. We'll confirm a delivery window during your consultation, and our team handles the placement and setup when we arrive.

Do I need power or internet at the site?

No. The tower runs on its rooftop solar array and battery pack, and it connects over built-in LTE. It's designed for raw jobsites and vacant lots with no utilities at all.

What's included in the $750/month tower rental?

The tower itself, delivery and deployment to your site, solar power with battery backup, LTE connectivity, and app access with live view and smart alerts. We service and support the unit for the life of your lease.

What does the $150/month monitoring actually do?

Professional monitoring agents review motion alerts from your site in real time, around the clock. When something looks wrong, they can speak to intruders through the tower's two-way audio, trigger the siren, and escalate to local authorities. One flat rate covers every tower on the site.

Can I watch the cameras myself?

Absolutely. You get live view and recorded motion events on your phone, tablet, or laptop, plus descriptive alerts like "a white truck is driving on the gravel area." You can share access with your site manager or foreman, too.

How long is the lease?

Leases are monthly and flexible, so you can cover a two-month project or a year-long build. We'll go over the terms that fit your timeline during your consultation.

What happens in bad weather or on cloudy days?

The tower is built for outdoor deployment, and its battery pack keeps it running overnight and through cloudy stretches. If a unit ever needs attention, our team comes out and takes care of it.

Where do you deliver?

Houston and the surrounding area, including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Pasadena, Baytown, Cypress, Spring, Humble, League City, Conroe, and more. Outside that radius? Ask us; we can often make it work.
